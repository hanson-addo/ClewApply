# Home: how it compares

Step 3 of #119. These notes compare the signed-in Home with four dashboards people already like. They come from public product pages and general knowledge of each product, not from a formal study.

| | Linear | Notion | Teal | Huntr | Clew Home |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Greets you and says what matters today | Inbox count | Plain greeting | Yes | No | Greeting plus a status line ("1 interview in play") |
| Numbers at a glance | Cycle stats | No | Job tracker counts | Board counts | Four tiles that count up and link to the right page |
| Next best action | Triage | No | Checklist | No | Setup checklist with a progress ring, then top matches |
| Ranked jobs on the first screen | n/a | n/a | No, you search | No, you add | Top 4 matches by fit, with pay |
| Pipeline overview | n/a | n/a | Kanban | Kanban | Bar per stage, links to the board |
| Motion | Subtle | Little | Little | Little | Rise-in, ring fill, count-up, arrow nudge. All off under reduced motion |
| Empty states | Illustrated | Plain text | Plain text | Illustrated | Yarn art and one clear next step |

## Where we match or beat them

- **Fit first.** Teal and Huntr track jobs you bring in. Clew shows ranked matches as soon as you sign in.
- **One headline that changes with you.** New users get "Let's get you hired" and a large resume button. Returning users get their name and the most important fact about their search.
- **Calm motion.** It's in the range of Linear's: short, eased, and never in the way.

## Where we're behind (later work)

- No daily digest or "new since your last visit" yet (phase 5 in `todo.md`).
- No customizable layout like Notion's. Not planned for the MVP.
- The jobs map is still a teaser (steps 4 and 5 of #119).
